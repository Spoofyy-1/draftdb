# Literature-Sourced Predictors of NBA Success Not Yet in the Model
Scope: peer-reviewed sports economics / sports science, MIT Sloan papers, and rigorous public analytics (Pelton, Vashro, Nylon Calculus, Silver/PRISM, Engelmann, EvanMiya). Ranked by expected contribution to Spearman-vs-realized-value on 2019–2025 classes.

### One-line feature definitions
1. `col_rapm_off/def` — ridge-regularized adjusted plus-minus per 100 possessions from reconstructed college lineup stints, shrunk to a box-score prior.
2. `tmate_adj_usage`, `tmate_quality_idx` — player's usage/scoring residual after partialling out sum of teammates' pre-draft consensus rank; plus that teammate-quality sum itself.
3. `top100_bpm_delta`, `tourney_resid` — (BPM vs top-100 opponents − BPM overall); and (post-Selection-Sunday production − prediction from pre-tourney consensus + season line).
4. `liwc_focusfuture`, `liwc_focuspresent`, `liwc_conj`, `liwc_authentic`, `liwc_emo_pos`, `liwc_verb`, `has_transcript` — dictionary word-rates over pre-draft-dated interview text, plus a missingness flag.
5. `hs_multisport` — 1 if any archived pre-draft bio names a second varsity HS sport.
6. `coach_effect_lag` — EB-shrunk coach intercept fit only on classes drafted before year *t*.
7. `games_missed_rate_y1/y2` — 1 − (player appearances / team games) per college season.
8. `birth_county_income_pctile`, `birth_metro_pop_band`.

## B. Cheap re-specifications of features you already have
| Idea | Evidence | Hrs |
|---|---|---|
| **Reverse-weight college seasons.** Pelton weights the most recent season **2×**, prior season **3×**, two seasons back **5×** — earlier seasons predict NBA outcomes better ([ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/16235135/explaining-kevin-pelton-nba-draft-projection-system)). | Pelton (WARP draft system) | 1 |
| **Conference fixed effects on top of SOS.** Adding age/height/weight **plus NCAA conference** took the Sloan model 83% → **87%**. | Sloan 2025 | 1 |
| **Position-relative normalization of every rate.** LUCARIO normalizes all numeric variables to positional means; Pelton regresses to positional rookie means, not global means ([Nylon](https://fansided.com/2018/06/11/nylon-calculus-nba-draft-model-process-lucario/)). | Nylon Calculus / Pelton | 2 |
| **Combine "length-size" composite instead of raw anthropometrics.** PCA subscale of height-no-shoes + standing reach + weight + wingspan + hand length was the only combine component significantly associated with WS/BPM/VORP; r=0.313–0.545 with DBPM ([Teramoto et al., *JSCR* 2018, n=234 of 1,092 combine participants 2010–15](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28135222/)). | Teramoto | 2 |
| **Model-minus-consensus deviation feature.** Berger (2021) finds managers "do not deviate enough from pre-draft rankings," systematically over/undervaluing groups ([MDE](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/mde.3305)). Encode the *gap*, not just the level. | Berger 2021 | 1 |

## C. What the literature says to discount
| Finding | Evidence | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Combine athletic tests (vertical, lane agility, sprint, bench) carry little signal.** Only the length-size subscale and, weakly, upper-body strength predicted WS/BPM/VORP; power-quickness did not. Silver's PRISM: combine measurements correlate with NBA success "not really." | Teramoto 2018; PRISM | Downweight/regularize your athletic tests hard; keep anthropometrics. |
| **Raw college scoring volume is what teams chase, not what predicts.** A 1-SD rise in points per 40 moves a player ~**6 draft slots** earlier; the authors argue this focus impairs bad teams. | Berri, Brook & Fenn, *J. Prod. Anal.* 35(1):25–35 (2011) ([link](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11123-010-0187-x)); see also Greer, Price & Berri, *IJSF* 14(1) 2019 ([link](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.32731/IJSF.141.202019.04)) | Penalize raw PPG; favor efficiency, creation, and defensive events. |
| **Jump shooting is the least stable translation.** Vashro: bad shooters become good shooters far more often than bad shot-blockers become good ones. | Vashro (DraftExpress/FanSided) | Shrink college 3P% aggressively toward an FT%+volume prior; do not shrink STL/BLK. |
| **Relative age effect is null in the NBA.** 2026 study: no significant birth-month skew, late-borns not taller/heavier/more likely centers, no draft-outcome difference; earlier work found Q1/Q2 over-represented in the *draft* but **no career performance difference**. | [Front. Sports Act. Living 2026](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2026.1787778/full); [PMC8019932](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8019932/) | Skip birth-quarter. Precise age already carries the signal. |
| **March Madness is *not* overvalued** — the opposite of the folk wisdom. | Ichniowski & Preston 2012/2017 | Add the tourney-residual feature (item A3) rather than de-weighting late-season data. |
| **Betting/prediction markets on draft slot** are only well-archived post-~2018 (Polymarket/Kalshi era) | — | Not obtainable for 2000–2017; skip. |

## D. Sequencing recommendation
Build **A3 (opponent-tier splits + tourney residual)** and **A7 (games missed)** first — both fall out of one `ncaahoopR_data` ingest, ~15 hours total, near-zero leakage. Then **A2** (teammate adjustment, reuses the same rosters). **A1 (college RAPM)** is the largest single bet: same data ingest, 25–40 hours, unvalidated in public literature but the only feature class that adds genuinely non-box-score information. **A4 (interview language)** is the most differentiated and has the strongest published lift, but coverage will be the binding constraint — build the missingness indicator in from day one.
